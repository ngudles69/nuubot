# AGENTS.md

Rules for files under `.project/plans/**`.

## Purpose

- This folder holds transient ExecPlans.
- Plans can be out of date.
- Plans guide work; they are not durable facts.

## Style

- Keep plans self-contained enough to resume the task.
- Record progress, surprises, decisions, and proof while the plan is active.
- Promote durable conclusions to `wiki/**`.
