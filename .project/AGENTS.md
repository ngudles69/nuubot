# AGENTS.md

Rules for files under `.project/**`.

## Purpose

- `.project/**` holds project management: active plans, roadmap notes, proof
  notes, and work tracking.
- `.project/**` can be out of date.
- `.project/**` is not project truth.
- Durable facts and decisions belong in `wiki/**`.

## Plans

- Put ExecPlans under `.project/plans/**`.
- Use ExecPlans only for multi-file, risky, or unclear implementation.
- Keep conclusions out of plans once they become durable; move them to
  `wiki/**`.
