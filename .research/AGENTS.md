# AGENTS.md

Rules for files under `.research/**`.

## Purpose

- `.research/**` holds transient project facts, investigation notes, raw
  evidence, and paths being explored for a specific action.
- `.research/**` facts are valid only for their specific task.
- `.research/**` can be out of date.
- `.research/**` is not project truth.
- Durable project knowledge belongs in `wiki/**`.

## Names

- Research files use `MMDD-<topic>-<vX>.md`.
- `vX` is the version, for example `v1`, `v2`, `v3`.
- Highest `vX` is the latest version for that topic and date.
